General Hindi (Paper 1)
  • व्याकरण, अपठित गद्यांश, संक्षेपण, पत्राचार/प्रारूप से जुड़े वर्णनात्मक प्रश्न (अधिसूचना अनुसार)
  • Qualifying प्रकृति — न्यूनतम अंक अनिवार्य
Essay (Paper 2)
  • सामाजिक, राजनीतिक, आर्थिक, पर्यावरणीय व समसामयिक विषयों पर निबंध
  • स्पष्ट संरचना: भूमिका → तर्क/उदाहरण → निष्कर्ष
GS I — History, Culture, Society, Geography
  • Indian culture — art forms, literature, architecture
  • Modern Indian history & freedom struggle
  • Post-independence consolidation (as in syllabus)
  • Indian society, women, urbanisation, globalisation effects
  • World & Indian physical/human/economic geography; UP geography overlap
GS II — Constitution, Governance, Social Justice, IR
  • Indian Constitution — features, amendments, basic structure
  • Parliament, State legislatures, Executive, Judiciary
  • Federalism, local governance, statutory bodies
  • Welfare schemes, governance, transparency
  • India & neighbourhood / international institutions (as listed)
GS III — Economy, Agriculture, Environment, S&T, Security
  • Indian economy, budgeting, growth, inclusive development
  • Agriculture, MSP, subsidies, food processing
  • Environment, biodiversity, disaster management
  • Science & technology applications; internal security basics
GS IV — Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude
  • Ethics & human interface; attitude; emotional intelligence
  • Public service values — integrity, impartiality, objectivity
  • Probity in governance; case studies
GS V — Uttar Pradesh Special (History, Culture, Governance)
  • UP history, art, culture, architecture
  • UP polity/governance, security, health, education
  • Current affairs of UP; NGOs, tourism, innovation (as per notification topics)
GS VI — Uttar Pradesh Special (Economy, Geography, Resources)
  • UP economy, schemes, agriculture
  • UP geography, demography, natural resources
  • Transport, energy, PPP / development issues of UP
