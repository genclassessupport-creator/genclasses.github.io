Paper I — General Studies

1. Current events (राष्ट्रीय व अंतर्राष्ट्रीय)
  • National & international current affairs
  • Government schemes, reports, summits (as asked in paper)
2. History of India & Indian National Movement
  • Broad social, economic, political aspects of Indian History
  • Freedom movement — nature, nationalism, Independence
3. India & World Geography
  • Physical, Social, Economic Geography of India
  • World Geography — general understanding
4. Indian Polity & Governance
  • Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj
  • Public Policy, Rights Issues
5. Economic & Social Development
  • Sustainable Development, Poverty, Inclusion
  • Demographics, Social Sector Initiatives
6. Environment, Ecology, Biodiversity & Climate Change
  • General issues — no subject specialisation required
7. General Science
  • Everyday science & basic concepts (non-technical level as in Prelims)

Paper II — CSAT (GS II)

Comprehension & Communication
  • Comprehension
  • Interpersonal skills including communication skills
  • General English (Class X level)
  • General Hindi (Class X level)
Reasoning & Decision Making
  • Analytical ability & logical reasoning
  • Problem solving & decision making
  • General mental ability
Maths & Data
  • Elementary Mathematics (Class X) — Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics
  • Basic numeracy & data interpretation (charts/tables)
