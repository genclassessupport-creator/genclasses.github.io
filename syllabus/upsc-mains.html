Essay

Multiple essays; topics from socio-economic, political, philosophical, contemporary themes as set by UPSC.

GS I — Culture, History, Society, Geography
  • Indian culture — art forms, literature, architecture
  • Modern Indian history; freedom struggle; post-independence consolidation
  • History of the world (industrial revolution, world wars, colonisation, redrawal of boundaries, etc. as in syllabus)
  • Indian society; diversity; women; poverty; urbanisation; globalisation
  • World & Indian geography — physical, resources, industries, geophysical phenomena
GS II — Polity, Governance, Social Justice, IR
  • Constitution, Parliament, Judiciary, federalism, local bodies
  • Statutory/regulatory/quasi-judicial bodies; governance; transparency; e-governance
  • Welfare schemes; social justice; vulnerable sections
  • International relations; bilateral/regional/global groupings; diaspora; international institutions
GS III — Economy, Agriculture, Environment, S&T, Security
  • Indian economy; budgeting; inclusive growth; infrastructure
  • Agriculture; food security; land reforms; LPG effects
  • Science & technology; awareness in IT, space, computers, robotics, nano, biotechnology
  • Environment; biodiversity; disaster management
  • Internal security; border management; cyber security; money laundering linkages
GS IV — Ethics, Integrity, Aptitude
  • Ethics and Human Interface; attitude; emotional intelligence
  • Moral thinkers; public service values; civil service aptitude
  • Probity in governance; RTI; citizen charters; corruption challenges
  • Case studies on above issues
