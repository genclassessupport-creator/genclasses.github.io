Six official areas
  • Opposites (विलोम)
  • Sentences and Correction in Framing (वाक्य रचना/शुद्धि)
  • One Word for Several Words (अनेक शब्दों के लिए एक शब्द)
  • Same Uses and Same Nature Words
  • Noun as defined by Adjective / Adjectives (विशेष्य–विशेषण)
  • Synonymous Words (पर्यायवाची)

Mains GS I — History, Society, Geography, Environment, S&T
  • Indian History, Art & Culture; Modern India 1757–1947; Freedom Struggle; post-independence to 1965
  • Indian Society — women, population, urbanisation, LPG effects, social justice
  • Geography of India (physical/economic/human) + Geography of Uttar Pradesh
  • Environment & Disaster Management — cycles, pollution, NDMA/NDRF/SDRF, epidemics
  • Science & Technology — space, IT, missiles, nano, AI/robotics, nuclear, biotech (as listed in advt.)
Mains GS II — Polity, Governance, Economy, Ethics, Current Affairs
  • Constitution, federalism, Parliament/State legislatures, judiciary, constitutional posts, NITI Aayog etc.
  • Governance, PIL, policies, ICT in implementation
  • Economy & Agriculture — SDGs, budget, subsidies, MSP, UP agriculture issues, tech missions
  • Ethics & Integrity — Indian ethical thought, utilitarianism/deontology, EI, public service values
  • Current Affairs — Global, South Asia/SAARC, India, Uttar Pradesh, Sports
Paper III — General Hindi and Drafting (mark-wise)
  • Unseen passage (title, explanation, précis) — 30
  • Govt letter summary in tabular form — 10
  • Correspondence (official/demi-official, memo, circular, noting, reports…) — 20
  • Admin/commercial terminology H↔E — 20
  • Idioms & proverbs — 30
  • Grammar (noun, pronoun, adjective, case, verb) — 15; Punctuation — 5; Error correction — 10
  • Varna Vichar — 10; Tatsam/Tadbhav/Deshaj/Videshi — 10; Sandhi/Samas/affixes — 10
  • Vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms, one-word, etc.) — 20; Computer GK — 10
Paper IV — Hindi Essay

Section I (80 marks · ~800 words): Science/Environment/Technology; Economic field; Agriculture/Industry/Trade; Natural disasters.

Section II (120 marks · ~1200 words): Literature & Culture; Social & Political; National & International events; National development schemes/projects.
