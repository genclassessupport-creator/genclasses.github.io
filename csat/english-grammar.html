उदाहरण
The list of items are on the desk.
→ Subject = list (singular) → is on the desk.
Error: are → is (S–V agreement)
Q1. Neither of the answers ___ correct.
उत्तर: is
Neither + singular verb।
  • गलत विकल्प क्यों / Why wrong:
  • Subject-verb disagreement।
  • Subject-verb disagreement.
Q2. She is senior ___ me.
उत्तर: to
senior/junior/superior + to।
  • गलत विकल्प क्यों / Why wrong:
  • Pronoun antecedent गलत।
  • Bad pronoun antecedent.
Q3. One of my friends ___ going abroad.
उत्तर: is
One of + plural noun + singular verb।
  • गलत विकल्प क्यों / Why wrong:
  • Modifier misplacement।
  • Misplaced modifier.
Q4. He insisted ___ paying the bill.
उत्तर: on
insist on + gerund।
  • गलत विकल्प क्यों / Why wrong:
  • Parallelism टूटा।
  • Broken parallelism.
Q5. Each of the girls ___ a book.
उत्तर: has
Each → singular।
  • गलत विकल्प क्यों / Why wrong:
  • Conditional रूप गलत।
  • Wrong conditional form.
